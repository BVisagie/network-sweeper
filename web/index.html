    How scanning works

    We first look for devices on common “discovery” ports. Then we check a broader list of ports on devices we found to label services and suggest risks. Quiet devices on your local network are also picked up from your computer’s ARP cache; others may need Deep discovery (ping; ARP on elevated Linux/macOS).
